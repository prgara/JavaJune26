2. Cloud Computing 


Flavours to "Someone else manages it"
- IaaS ---> Infrastructure as a service  ..... AWS EC2
you manage  ----   OS, app, data
they manage ----   physical servers, networking

- PaaS ----> Platform as a Service  ........ Shopify, OpenShift
you manage ----- add, data
they manage ----- everything they manage except above

- SaaS  ----> Software as a service ....... Gmail, Neflix, whats-app
you manage ---- nothing , just your account
they manage ---- the entire stack
